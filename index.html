About Catherine
CM
  • Native French speaker
  • EEE — highest proficiency level
  • 15+ years in federal government
  • Lived and studied in Germany
  • One-on-one sessions only
  • Virtual via Microsoft Teams

Some people learn languages in classrooms. Catherine learned them by living.

Growing up in a military family, home was never one place for long. By the time she was a teenager, she had landed in Germany — spending four formative years studying in French, English, and German, surrounded by people from every corner of the world. Languages weren't a subject. They were survival, connection, and sometimes, frustration.

That experience taught her something no classroom could: what it truly feels like to not be understood — and how much it matters to have a safe space to try anyway.

Catherine's sessions are judgment-free by design. Her students come in nervous and leave at ease — because she has been exactly where they are.

Today, Catherine brings over 15 years of federal government experience and a firsthand understanding of what it means to work in a bilingual environment. She saw the frustration of people who had been through the system and come out defeated — budgets that didn't stretch far enough, teachers whose style didn't click, programs that moved at someone else's pace. People who had tried, felt lost, and quietly given up.

She built something different.

Before achieving EEE — the highest rating across all three components of the government language evaluation: reading, writing, and oral — she sat the oral exam three times. On her own. No tutor, no program, no safety net. That journey is exactly why she teaches the way she does.
